# Legal Notice

VANTA performs **active network interception**. Every attack it ships
is a hostile action on any network you do not own.

## You may use VANTA only:

- On networks **you own** (your home lab, your test VLAN).
- In **isolated lab environments** (Linux namespaces, VMs, containers).
- On **engagements with written authorization** that explicitly names
  network interception as in-scope.

## You may not use VANTA:

- On public Wi-Fi.
- On a corporate, university, or ISP network without written permission.
- Against friends, family, or "just to see if it works."
- To intercept traffic of any person who has not consented.

## Legal consequences

Unauthorized interception is a criminal offense in nearly every
jurisdiction, including but not limited to:

- **United States** — Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (18 U.S.C. § 1030),
  Wiretap Act (18 U.S.C. § 2511)
- **United Kingdom** — Computer Misuse Act 1990, Investigatory Powers Act
- **European Union** — Directive 2013/40/EU, national implementations
- **Pakistan** — Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016
- **Germany** — StGB § 202a, § 202b, § 303a
- **India** — Information Technology Act 2000, § 43, § 66

Penalties range from fines to multi-year imprisonment.
